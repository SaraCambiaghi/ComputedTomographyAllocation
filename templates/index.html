CT Exam Scheduling for Outpatients

Disclaimer: This tool is intended for illustrative purposes. Once a set of patients and their characteristics is entered, the tool predicts the duration of their examinations or report, depending on the selected criterion, and produces a weekly schedule designed to balance the daily workload and evenly distribute patients according to their type (in this case, body or neuro). We aim to minimize the maximum daily workload, and we aim also to keep body and nauro patients more or less in the same number each day, Note: both the features used for the predictions and the computation time allocated to the solver for finding the optimal schedule are limited due to computational constraints. The purpose of this tool is not to provide a deployable solution, but rather to demonstrate a possible application of predictions generated through machine learning algorithms.
Auto-generated patients: Patient records have been automatically generated using realistic data derived from our dataset. All fields may be modified as necessary prior to submission; however, please ensure that the data remains realistic to allow the tool to function correctly.
The algorithm may take a few minutes to process the data and compute the schedule. Please wait until the page refreshes automatically.
